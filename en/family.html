Guide to preparing a UK spouse or family visa

A visa for living in the UK with a partner or family. This page organises the conditions, evidence and preparation steps to check before you apply.

UK immigration rules can change. This page is general information and is not advice on your own case. Before you apply, read the latest UK government information.

01. Check which route you are looking at

A family visa is not one set of conditions for everyone. The conditions differ with your partner's status in the UK, your relationship, and where you apply from.

What to checkWhat it covers
Your partner's status in the UKBritish or Irish citizenship, settled status, or another status the route covers
Your relationshipMarriage, a civil partnership, or another lasting partner relationship the route covers
Where you plan to liveWhether you plan to live together in the UK
Other points on the official pageIncome, English, housing, and previous immigration history

02. The main kinds of documents

These are general groups. They are not a list that every applicant submits. What applies depends on the route, the type of income, and your residence history.

Type of documentExamplesWhat to look at
Identity ↗A valid passport, and previous travel or residence informationWhether names and earlier applications match

Income or funds ↗Payslips, bank statements, or a letter from an employerWhether it matches the income category on the official page

03. How the preparation moves

  1. Check the route and the conditions:Use the UK government guidance to identify your route.
  2. Sort the evidence:List identity, relationship, income and the other documents for your route.
  3. Look for gaps and mismatches:Check names, dates, the period covered, and whether a translation is included.
  4. Apply on the official site:Complete the online application for your route and submit documents as the guidance describes.
  5. Identity check, then the decision:Provide biometric information where the process asks for it, and wait for the decision.
The visa application itself follows the UK government's process. A private app does not replace the government system or legal advice.

05. Common questions

Does being married mean you can apply?

Marriage on its own does not decide it. Check your partner's status, the relationship, English and funds against the route on GOV.UK.

Can a certificate in another language be used as it is?

Documents that are not in English or Welsh may need a certified translation that meets the official rules. Check the official guidance.

Does using Stratovisa mean the visa is granted?

No. The UK authorities decide the application. Using a document-preparation service does not guarantee a grant.
