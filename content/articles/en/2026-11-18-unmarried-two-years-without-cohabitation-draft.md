When applying as an unmarried partner, a two-year relationship and two years at the same address are different questions. [GOV.UK’s family visa guidance](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/partner-spouse) covers couples who have been in a relationship for at least two years but cannot live together, for example because they work or study in different places. It asks for evidence of an ongoing commitment to each other.

The Home Office looks for a relationship comparable to marriage or a civil partnership that has lasted at least two years before you apply. The date you first met may be different from the date that kind of relationship began. [Home Office caseworker guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/family-life-as-a-partner-or-parent-private-life-and-exceptional-circumstance/family-life-as-a-partner-or-parent-and-exceptional-circumstances-accessible)

## Work out what each record shows

If you lived together in the UK for part of the time, a **Council Tax bill** from your local council may help document that period if it confirms that you lived at the same address or paid bills together. A bill in one person’s name for a period spent apart can help you check that person’s address, but does not by itself show what the relationship was like. [GOV.UK’s examples of relationship evidence](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/partner-spouse)

For the period apart, make a short timeline. A university letter or a letter from an employer can help explain why one of you was based elsewhere. Travel bookings and dated records of visits can help show when you spent time together. The public guidance also gives examples such as regular contact, financial support and looking after children you have together, where these apply. [GOV.UK guidance on living apart](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/partner-spouse#if-you-do-not-live-together)

## Keep the dates and translations together

Label each record with the period it covers and what it helps explain. If a document you submit is in a language other than English or Welsh, [GOV.UK asks for a certified translation](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/provide-information). Keep the original and its translation together so that names and dates are easy to compare.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a Council Tax bill cover the time we lived apart?

A bill is useful for the dates and address it actually records. For the time apart, use records that explain why you lived separately and how you stayed involved in each other’s lives. [GOV.UK’s partner application guide](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/partner-spouse) sets out the other requirements.
